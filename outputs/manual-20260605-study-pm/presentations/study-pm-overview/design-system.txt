Background system:
- Warm paper base #F7F2EA with dark ink chapter moments #111827.

Typography:
- Japanese sans: Hiragino Sans / Yu Gothic fallback through artifact runtime.
- Claim titles use large bold sans, not decorative serif, because Japanese readability is more important than display flourish.

Palette:
- Ink #111827 for primary text.
- Soft text #687386.
- Blue #2563EB as app accent and action path.
- Amber #D08A22 as warning / delay / decision accent.
- Green #14A76C as completion / positive progress.
- Line #D9D6CE for quiet structure.
- Paper #F7F2EA and white #FFFFFF.

Chart grammar:
- Direct labels; no heavy legends.
- Use horizontal bars and compact rails for sample metrics.
- Metrics are grounded in mock data and labeled as sample state.

Diagram grammar:
- Nodes represent concrete app objects: Project, WBS, Study Log, EVM, Questions, AI Plan.
- Blue connectors represent primary workflow; amber connectors represent warnings/feedback.
- Avoid decorative arrows unless direction matters.

Container grammar:
- Use thin lines and subtle fills; no repeated generic card grid.
- Equal-role nodes share sizes and alignment.

Footer/page marker:
- Quiet source footer at bottom-left.
- Two-digit page marker bottom-right.

Title/kicker:
- Kicker uses small marker square plus letter-spaced English label.
- Claim title states a conclusion, not a topic name.

Brand asset policy:
- No official logo is present; no pseudo-logo or app icon will be created.
- Allowed brand cues are app name text, blue accent, dark sidebar-like background, and product vocabulary.

Layout families:
- Cover with outcome rail.
- Fragmentation map.
- Product system map.
- Journey flow.
- Roadmap with screen coverage rail.
- Metric proof slide.
- Platform direction map.
- Decision stack.

Banned motifs:
- Generic SaaS feature-card grids.
- Fake logos, mascots, app icons, or official-looking marks.
